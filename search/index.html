Matching is exact language, paragraph by paragraph, across every talk from 1830 to the present — no silent stemming, no guessing. Known archaic spellings (shew/show, labour/labor, Sion/Zion) are searched together and declared in the reading line; word forms broadens to stemmed matches when you ask. Narrow by era, decade, or calling in the left rail; order by relevance or by date; concordance lines up every hit keyword-in-context, the classical instrument. Each result opens the talk at the cited paragraph, and the address bar always holds a link to the search itself, so a search can be shared like any citation. Looking to browse rather than search?

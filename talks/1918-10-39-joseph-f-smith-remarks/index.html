I have only one regret, and

Joseph F. Smith, President of the Church

1918 October General Conference·Second Outdoor Meeting·1918-10-06

I have only one regret, and that is that this day could not be lengthened out some six or eight or twelve hours longer, so that we could hear from the rest of the brethren who are full of the spirit of their missions and of the Lord, and that you, one and all of you, would be delighted to hear. But the time will not permit. ¶

A sacred solo, "The Ninety-and-nine," was sung by Edna Chipman, just returned from the Eastern States Mission. ¶

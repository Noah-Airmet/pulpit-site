My Brethren and Sisters, This Session...

J. Reuben Clark, First Counselor in the First Presidency

1960 April General Conference·Fourth Day Morning Meeting·1960-04-06

My brethren and sisters, this session of our General Conference is in some respects the most essential feature of the Conference. We who are here today are the Church in Conference assembled. We are a constituent assembly of the Church, acting as the Church, and your votes today are the votes of the Church. As I present the officers I wish you to understand that as you vote for the sustaining or otherwise of the officers, you vote as the Church. You make a solemn obligation, as you vote as the Church that you will sustain and uphold the officers of the Church in the positions into which they come as the result of your approving vote. There is no appeal provided for from your decisions, except the appeal that would go to our Heavenly Father if we did anything wrong. ¶

I will now present, as has been announced, the following persons for the positions in which you will vote to sustain or otherwise, of this Church. ¶

Remarks

Marriner W. Merrill, Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

1896 October General Conference·Tuesday Morning Session·1896-10-06

[Summary from Deseret Weekly proceedings: Elder Marriner W. Merrill said he knew there was an evil power as well as one which was good abroad among the people, and related experiences of his boyhood demonstrating the existence of such influences. He had been given an evidence and testimony that the Lord would come to his rescue. Subsequently, before he heard the Gospel, he had evidence of its existence in an open vision in the day time. He saw the Church, the Prophet Joseph, the Elders of Israel, the trials of the Saints and their gathering in these valleys. He exhorted the Saints to faithfulness in the payment of their tithes and offerings.] ¶

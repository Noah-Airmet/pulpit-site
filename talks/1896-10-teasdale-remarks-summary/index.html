Remarks

George Teasdale, Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

1896 October General Conference·Sunday Overflow Meeting·1896-10-04

[Summary from Deseret Weekly proceedings: Elder George Teasdale spoke of the restoration of the Gospel of Christ through Joseph Smith the Prophet, and the present condition of the world, which was steeped in wickedness. The Saints were in possession of the truth, which would regenerate them. They had light and darkness, truth and error presented for their acceptance, and if they chose the former it would lead them up to the presence of God. The slothful and indifferent among them would not be accounted worthy to stand.] ¶

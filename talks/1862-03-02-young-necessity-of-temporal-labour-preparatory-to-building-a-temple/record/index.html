Necessity of Temporal Labour, Preparatory to Building a Temple

Brigham Young · · 1862-03-02

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 9, pp. 239-241. Reported by G. D. Watt; the speaker did not review this text before publication. Header confirmed from the discourse's own byline: 'Remarks made by President Brigham Young, Tabernacle, Great Salt Lake City, March 2, 1862. Reported by G. D. Watt.' March 2, 1862 does not fall inside the 1862 General Conference window (April 6-9) in indexes/conference-calendar.md; category set tabernacle_address; occasion recorded as the printed place, Tabernacle, Great Salt Lake City. The printed text contains an internal editorial break ('I will now call upon Bishop Hunter to make some remarks.' followed by a printed horizontal rule, then Young resuming without a new byline) marking that Bishop Hunter's own remarks were not transcribed for this printed entry; rendered as a thematic break per docs/qc-pipeline.md's markup rule rather than treated as a second discourse or a boundary gap.

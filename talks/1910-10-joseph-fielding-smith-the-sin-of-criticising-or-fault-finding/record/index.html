The sin of criticising or fault finding

Joseph Fielding Smith · 1910 October General Conference · 1910-10-07

Fidelity

◕Authoritative print

Transcribed from the OCR of the official printed Conference Report of the Eighty-First Semi-Annual Conference (October 6, 7, and 9, 1910), published by the Deseret News and hosted at the Internet Archive (conferencereport1910sa; DjVu file conferencereport811chur). Reading order was reconstructed from the DjVu word coordinates with scripts/ingest/cr-reading-order.py. The printed report supplies an editorial topic-summary for each discourse, preserved at the top of the body when present; the title is supplied from that summary. Not yet proofread line-by-line against the page images (status: draft, pending Final Review). source_archived_url points to the Internet Archive item itself.

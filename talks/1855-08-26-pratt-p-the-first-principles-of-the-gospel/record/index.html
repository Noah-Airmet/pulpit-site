The First Principles of the Gospel

Parley P. Pratt · · 1855-08-26

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 9, pp. 204-217 (printed out of chronological order among 1861-62 discourses; this address predates most of its neighbors in the printed volume by several years, mirroring jod-09-033 earlier in the same run of entries). Reported by J. V. Long; the speaker did not review this text before publication. Header confirmed from the discourse's own byline: 'Discourse by Elder Parley P. Pratt, delivered in the Bowery, Great Salt Lake City, Sunday Morning, August 26, 1855. Reported by J. V. Long.' August 26, 1855 does not fall inside a General Conference window in indexes/conference-calendar.md; category set tabernacle_address.

Amasa M. Lyman · · 1855-12-09

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 3, pp. 164-177 (delivered December 9, 1855). Printed byline: 'A Discourse, by President Amasa Lyman, Delivered in the Tabernacle, Great Salt Lake City, December 9, 1855.' The discourse opens by referring to the First Presidency's recent absence on a journey south (see jod-03-020, delivered by the same speaker three days earlier under similar circumstances).

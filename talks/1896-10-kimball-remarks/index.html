Remarks

Elias S. Kimball, Mission President (Southern States Mission)

1896 October General Conference·Tuesday Afternoon Session·1896-10-06

[Summary from Deseret Weekly proceedings: Elder Elias S. Kimball, President of the Southern States mission, said that his heart was in his missionary work, and he had labored earnestly with his 366 Elders to advance the work of God in the Southern States, under the inspiration of the Holy Ghost. New fields were being opened up and large cities visited. The thirteen conferences were yielding large returns to the systematic and organized labors of the Elders.] ¶

Session notes: music and prayer

Conference record

1919 October General Conference·First Day Afternoon Session·1919-10-03

SECOND OVERFLOW MEETING. The second overflow meeting was held at the Assembly Hall Sunday, at 2 p. m. October 5, 1919. Elder George F. Richards, of the Council of the Twelve Apostles, presided. Music was furnished by the Richards ward choir of the Granite Stake of Zion. ¶

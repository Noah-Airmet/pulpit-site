Remarks

Wilford Woodruff, President of the Church

1896 October General Conference·Sunday Morning Session·1896-10-04

[Summary from Deseret Weekly proceedings: President Wilford Woodruff testified to the testimony of this dispensation with all that had preceded it, in the gifts and blessings of the Spirit, naming instances of the exercises of such gifts. He also referred to the work for the dead, testifying that this labor is being accepted in heaven, and that Apostle Abraham H. Cannon and others had been called into the spirit world to perform missions there in connection with this work. Joseph Smith was the first since the days of the ancient Apostles to preach this doctrine.] ¶

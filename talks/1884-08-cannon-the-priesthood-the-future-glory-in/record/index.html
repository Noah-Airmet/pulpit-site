The Priesthood—The Future Glory in Store for All Those Who Receive and Magnify the Priesthood—Without the Priesthood "No Man Can See the Face of God, Even the Father and Live"—Moses and the Children of Israel

George Q. Cannon · · 1884-08-10

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 25, pp. 288-295, one of a series of talks printed together under the shared byline 'REMARKS ON VARIOUS SUBJECTS, BY PRESIDENT GEORGE Q. CANNON, Delivered (in the Settlements indicated) during the recent visit of President Taylor and party to the Northern Settlements. 1884. (REPORTED BY JOHN IRVINE.)' printed once at the head of the series (JD 25:288). Each stop's remarks in the series, including this one, is set off only by its own printed side-head and closes with its own bracketed place/date postscript rather than repeating the byline -- this entry closes '[The foregoing was delivered in Paris, Bear Lake County, Idaho, Sunday afternoon, August 10, 1884.]' (p.295), matching the packet's date_candidate. The discourse opens by reading and expounding Doctrine and Covenants 84:14-42 verbatim as printed; speaker did not review this text before publication.

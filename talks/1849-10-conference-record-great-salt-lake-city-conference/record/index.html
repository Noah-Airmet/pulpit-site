Minutes of the October 1849 Great Salt Lake City General Conference

Conference record · 1849 October General Conference · 1849-10-06

Fidelity

○Fragmentary

Full transcription pass (2026-07-24, claude-opus-5) of the *original* CHL minutes, the item the previous pass's Bullock copy pointed to with its own note 'See following for original minutes with more detail.' All 11 leaves of 'General Conference proceedings, Salt Lake City' (Historian's Office general Church minutes, 1839-1877, folder '1849 October 1-7', CR 100 318, Box 2 fd 15, recorded by Thomas Bullock) are now transcribed below by direct reading of the CHL leaf images, fetched via the catalog's own delivery API (accessLevelCode 'unconditional' confirmed on the item, and the parent folder carries restrictionLevel 'open to research', before download). Method - four overlapping 2x-scale contrast-enhanced bands per leaf plus targeted 4-5x zooms on hard words. Delivery order is not document order; the sequence was reconstructed from session headings, the ink leaf-numbering, and text continuity across leaf breaks, and is leaf 11 (Oct 6, 10 a.m.), 1, 2, 3 (Oct 6 candlelight officers' meeting), 4, 5 (Oct 7, 10 a.m.), 6, 7 (Oct 7, 2 p.m., then the evening setting-apart meeting), 8, 9, with leaf 10 the docketed wrapper (verso of leaf 9). The record covers four sessions plus the evening missionary setting-apart meeting at which each departing elder's blessing was taken down verbatim. Still graded fragmentary because Bullock's minute-hand carries word-level uncertainty throughout; uncertain readings are bracketed in place, cancellations recorded as [struck: ...], interlineations as ^[...], and expanded abbreviations in square brackets.

Apostacy the Result of Ignorance—True Government, Etc

Brigham Young · · 1858-06-06

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 7, pp. 63-67, printed as 'Remarks by President Brigham Young, delivered in the Bowery, Provo, June 6, 1858,' reported by J. B. Milner -- delivered during the Utah War 'Move South.' Printed title spells 'Apostacy' (not the modern 'Apostasy'); preserved as printed. Not General Conference (no GC window covers June 1858; see indexes/conference-calendar.md). Pitman shorthand report; speaker did not review this text before publication.

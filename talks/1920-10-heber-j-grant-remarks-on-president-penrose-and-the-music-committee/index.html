Remarks on President Penrose and the Music Committee

Heber J. Grant, President of the Church

1920 October General Conference·First Day Morning Session·1920-10-08

I am grateful beyond expression that President Penrose has been able to speak to us. He has been at home, sick abed, most of the time during the last week. ¶

I will state that a general music committee has been selected, and will undoubtedly be sustained at our conference, with Elder Mc J. Ballard as the head of that committee. ¶

Remarks

Francis M. Lyman, Quorum of the Twelve Apostles

1896 October General Conference·Monday Morning Session·1896-10-05

[Summary from Deseret Weekly proceedings: Elder Francis M. Lyman thought that the earnest testimony and exhortation of the inspired speakers of the Conference should imbue each individual with an assurance of the divinity of the work. He spoke of the Prophet Joseph having ushered in the dawn of spiritual day after the long night of darkness, and bore testimony of Joseph's divine mission. He emphasized the proper recognition of God's authority and exhorted all to remain in the light and inspiration of the Gospel of Christ.] ¶

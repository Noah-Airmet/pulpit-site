Summary of Instructions

Brigham Young · · 1865-07-01

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 11, pp. 110-119, under the printed heading "Summary of Instructions." Header reads "Given by President Brigham Young to the people, on his visit to Utah, Juab, and Sanpete Counties, in June and July, 1865. Reported by G. D. Watt." This is a composite record: the printed entry itself states it compiles remarks from a multi-stop tour rather than a single dated discourse (the body names June 27 as the day before the twenty-first anniversary of Joseph Smith's death, and separately recounts an incident at Mount Pleasant and Manti in San Pete County). date/date_precision therefore record the range as approximate rather than a single exact delivery date; the printed volume/page range is the authority for scope.

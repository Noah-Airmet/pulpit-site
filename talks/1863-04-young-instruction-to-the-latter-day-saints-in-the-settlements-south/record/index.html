Instruction to the Latter-Day Saints, in the Settlements South of Great Salt Lake City

Brigham Young · · 1863-04-20

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 10, pp. 221-229. Reported by G. D. Watt; the speaker did not review this text before publication. The printed header dates this compiled set of instructions 'in April and May, 1863,' and the body's own opening line anchors the first instruction to 'the 20th of April, 1863,' when President Young and company left Great Salt Lake City and arrived at American Fork; date recorded here as approximate, reflecting that anchor rather than a single fixed delivery date. Text transcribed from the printed JD.

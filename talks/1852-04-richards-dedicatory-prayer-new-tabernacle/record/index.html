Dedication Prayer

Willard Richards · 1852 April General Conference · 1852-04-06

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 6, pp. 298-306 (reported April 6, 1852), and in the Deseret News, April 17, 1852, p. 46. Pitman shorthand report by George D. Watt; the speaker did not review this text before publication. Text transcribed from the printed JD; restored to the complete unabridged prayer (see final_review_notes).

Collation history

Proofread against the JD scan witness; restored two previously-abridged passages. Superseded by the full page-by-page restoration in final_review_notes.

Collated word-by-word against the original scan.

Heavyweight pass: no official churchofjesuschrist.org or CHL digitization exists for this 1852 discourse; BYU ContentDM/IA JD 6:298-306 remains the best verified source. Body was flagged ABRIDGED by a prior pass; restored two missing passages verbatim confirmed against the source piecewise: (1) a 'lawyers not have power to stir up strife' clause in the civil-authorities blessing, and (2) the bulk of the Native-American ('ancient covenant people') passage, which had been trimmed to two sentences. This partial restoration was itself found still well under half the printed prayer by two later Final Review sessions and has now been superseded by a complete page-by-page restoration; see final_review_notes.

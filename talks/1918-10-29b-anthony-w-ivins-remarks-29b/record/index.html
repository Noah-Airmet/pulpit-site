Collation history

Newly ingested talk, previously missing from the vault entirely. Elder Ivins's short remarks on the Utah prohibition amendment, delivered at the Second Overflow Meeting (Assembly Hall, Sunday afternoon, Oct. 6, 1918, Elder Ivins presiding), had been wrongly spliced onto the tail of 1918-10-29-joseph-w-mcmurrin-remarks.md with no boundary marker; removed from that file, without being preserved elsewhere, in commit 5b1174eb83 (final-review: 1918-10 joseph-w-mcmurrin-29). Recovered from the '-' lines of that commit, then independently re-verified against a fresh djvu XML reading-order reconstruction of conferencereport892chur_djvu.xml (the file actually served under the conferencereport1918sa identifier) via scripts/ingest/cr-reading-order.py, and against the Internet Archive BookReader page image for leaf n111 (printed page 110). Both independent OCR extractions agreed on the text with only trivial line-break/whitespace differences; the only substantive fix was 'strug'gle' -> 'struggle'. Talk begins under its own byline 'ELDER ANTHONY W. IVINS' (no calling line printed — he was a sitting member of the Quorum of the Twelve, per this vault's existing 1918-10-12-anthony-w-ivins-remarks.md; calling_at_time reused from that file's chronology-sourced value) immediately after the session-opening housekeeping (meeting particulars, choir, prayer, sustaining vote), which is administrative content and not part of this discourse per docs/ingestion.md and so is not included in the body here. Ends cleanly with 'Amen.' on leaf n111, immediately before 'ELDER WM. H. MENDENHALL.' (1918-10-30-william-h-mendenhall-remarks.md) begins. Session/date corrected from the host file's now-superseded 'Fourth Day Morning Session' / 1918-10-04 to the corrected Second Overflow Meeting / 1918-10-06 established by that same final-review commit (there is no Fourth Day in this conference; Third Day is Sunday Oct. 6 and runs through the rest of the printed report). talk_order 29.5 / id suffix 29b follow the vault's established convention for inserting a discovered talk between two already-numbered talks without renumbering the rest of the conference (compare 1918-10-24b-stephen-l-richards-remarks-24b.md, 1918-10-17b-charles-w-nibley-remarks-17b.md, both on branch final-review/1918-10).

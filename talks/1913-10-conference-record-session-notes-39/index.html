Session notes: Third Day opening, music and prayer

Conference record

1913 October General Conference·Third Day Morning Session·1913-10-06

THIRD DAY. ¶

Conference was resumed in the Tabernacle, at 10 a. m., Monday, October 6th; President Joseph F. Smith presiding. ¶

The choir and congregation sang the hymn: ¶

How firm a foundation, ye Saints of the Lord, Is laid for your faith in His excellent word! What more can He say than to you He hath said, You who unto Jesus for refuge have fled? ¶

Prayer was offered by Elder James Wotherspoon. ¶

The choir and congregation sang the hymn: ¶

O, say, what is Truth? 'Tis the fairest gem That the riches of worlds can produce; And priceless the value of truth will be, when The proud monarch's costliest diadem Is counted but dross and refuse. ¶

Opposition to "Mormonism," Etc

George A. Smith · · 1857-11-15

Fidelity

◑Contemporaneous report

Published in Journal of Discourses, Volume 6, pp. 60-62. Reported by J. V. Long. Collated against the BYU JoD volume 6 page scan (IIIF leaves 67-69, printed pages 60-62). The two research-oracle copies matched each other and the print almost exactly (shared textual lineage); both silently Americanize the printed volume's British spelling ('neighbour', 'endeavouring') where the print gives the '-our' forms — restored the printed spelling in each case. Header verified against the printed page: title 'OPPOSITION TO "MORMONISM," ETC.', 'Remarks by Elder George A. Smith, made in the Tabernacle, Great Salt Lake City, Sunday Morning, November 15, 1857,' reported by J. V. Long. November 15, 1857 does not fall inside a General Conference window in indexes/conference-calendar.md (1857 GC windows: April 6-9, October 6-7); category set tabernacle_address. This entry shares printed page 60 with the immediately preceding catalog entry (jod-06-008); only this entry's own discourse (beginning at the 'OPPOSITION TO "MORMONISM," ETC.' heading) is transcribed here.
